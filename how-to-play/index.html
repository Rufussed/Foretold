How to Play Foretold

Foretold is a free online trick-taking card game for 3 to 6 players, based on Wizard and Oh Hell. Each round, you predict how many tricks you will win, then try to make it come true. Here are the full rules.

Play Foretold now Free in your browser, with friends or computer players.

Foretold was inspired by Wizard, the card game by Ken Fisher. It is an independent project and is not affiliated with or endorsed by AMIGO or the creators of Wizard.
