# Seer — character proposal

Status: shared direction for four initial generations.
Rank: 8, present in each element.

## Shared character

An elderly figure whose long attention to things unseen has made ordinary human
company feel unfamiliar. Gaunt, stooped and unnervingly still, with a deeply
lined face, sparse silver hair, heavy-lidded eyes and long deliberate fingers.
His expression mixes dread with a terrible certainty: he recognises something
in the vision that he hoped never to see again.

Make him creepy through attention, gesture and expression rather than gore.
His mouth might be slightly open as if answering someone who is not there.
His eyes should remain readable and expressive, rather than becoming blank
glowing effects.

He wears an intact, carefully kept ceremonial robe: severe, elegant and unusual
in its cut, with a high collar and broad areas of elemental colour. Restrained
ornament and one clearly integrated suit motif. Age and unease do not imply
ragged clothing.

Where the Trickster knowingly changes what others believe, the Seer is disturbed
by what he believes he has discovered. The scrying device should establish a
clear action and silhouette for each version.

## Four elemental readings

These are four different people, not recolours or repeated poses. Use related
species and elemental palettes to the approved Tricksters, but create original
costumes, facial identities, gestures and locations. Fire may be an aged horned
demon, Earth an ancient green-skinned forest elf, Water an old human, and Air
a different elderly human. All should feel older and creepier than their
Trickster counterparts.

- **Fire staging:** aged ash-skinned horned demon, narrow severe crimson
  ceremonial robe, standing in profile with bent back and both long hands
  surrounding a single flame in a low stone brazier. Volcanic shrine, red sky.
  Frightened gaze, small recoil; no seated Trickster pose or hidden ember.
- **Earth staging:** ancient green-skinned elf with long ears and sparse pale
  hair, heavy moss-green mantle, seated upright amid a hollow tree's roots.
  Both hands hold a smoky mineral orb close to one eye; head tilted, expression
  of grim recognition. No acorns or crouching shell-game pose.
- **Water staging:** elderly gaunt human, deep-blue high-collared ceremonial
  robe, kneeling beside a broad shallow stone basin in a tidal grotto. Body in
  three-quarter view, one hand grips the rim while the other finger hovers
  over the water. Face visible above basin, alarmed, not hidden by downward gaze.
- **Air staging:** thin elderly human with a long silver beard and ivory/slate
  layered robe, planted firmly on a high windswept observatory terrace, body
  turning away while head cranes toward a small circling flock. One arm raised
  naturally to track the birds, other holds robe at waist. Fearful recognition;
  no leaping or floating purse. Birds below the quiet upper corner zones.

| Element | Device | Action and emotional moment |
|---|---|---|
| Fire | Flame | Holds his palms around one tall, unnaturally steady flame in a small brazier. He recoils slightly while continuing to stare, horrified but unable to look away. |
| Earth | Orb | Cups a smoky rock-crystal orb, tilting it toward his face as if examining something buried impossibly deep inside. His grip tightens; his expression becomes grimly certain. |
| Water | Basin | Bends over a broad, shallow basin of dark water. One finger hovers just above the unbroken surface, arrested by a revelation; his raised eyes and tense mouth show alarm. |
| Air | Flock of birds | Reads the pattern of a small, clearly grouped flock circling through the open sky. He cranes his neck and raises one crooked finger to follow their movement, whispering with fearful recognition. |

The Earth orb is mineral and weighty; the Air reading uses moving living signs.
These keep the devices distinct while fitting each element naturally.

## Visual treatment

Tarot-inspired painterly forms with expressive dark ink contours. Broad coherent
colour blocks, few shadow shapes, subtle surface texture. Keep the face, hands
and scrying device readable at playing-card size.

Carry each suit's restricted palette through robe, sky and surroundings.
Keep both upper corners quiet for rank numbers. For Air, position the flock in
a clear band below those corner zones; use a few distinct bird silhouettes,
not a cloud of tiny marks. For Water, tilt the basin enough that its surface is
visible without hiding the Seer's face.

Keep magical effects restrained. The character's response should be more
unsettling than an elaborate apparition. No extra faces, monsters or narrative
scenes inside the devices unless deliberately proposed later.

## Starting point

Fire offers a strong first study: the gaunt face and long fingers surround one
simple flame, creating a clear, intimate focal arrangement. A dark crimson robe,
pale face, black ink shapes and a small ivory-gold flame can remain readable
with very little tonal complexity.
