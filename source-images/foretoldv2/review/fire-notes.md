# Fire batch visual review

Reviewed 3 November 2026. Contact sheet order is manifest order, five columns:
Peasant, Farmer, Citizen, Soldier, Merchant / Priest, General, Thief, Trickster,
Seer / Heir, Consort, Monarch, Wizard, Fool.

All fifteen full-size outputs were inspected individually and then as a contact
sheet. Each has a paired exact prompt history and individual QA JSON. Original
sources remain untouched. Strong irregular ink and broad shading/landscape
masses are consistent across the set. The Monarch required the largest change
from realistic source rendering; original pose, enormous red horns, staff and
crescent bowl remain recognisable. Ember/crimson skies stay consistent for
suited cards. Wizard retains its original volcanic story while elementless;
Fool retains original pale sky/mountains and no Fire identity.

Conspicuous emblems were removed. Thirteen suited cards have four visible
strokes after corrections where necessary. Seer and Consort use somewhat
lighter red thread than requested but remain small tonal details. Monarch's
motif moved from gold cuff to red cloth after repeated three-stroke results.
Wizard/Fool have no suit motifs. Fool's floral costume remains colourful with
the former four conspicuous suit symbols removed.

Fresh Fire Trickster was created without viewing the old Fire Trickster or its
prompts: human-looking theatrical deceiver with no horns or copied scar,
manipulating dragon shadow puppet and lantern. Disconnected first-pass props
were corrected. The projected dragon shadow is theatrical, not an optical
reconstruction. No Water mask copied.

Major anatomy/object issues found during generation were corrected: Seer's near
hand now has five digits; General/Heir motifs corrected; Trickster props now
connect. Wizard's outstretched fingers partly overlap, so exact digit separation
is less explicit than Seer's corrected hands. No major remaining broken weapon
axes, floating hands or detached props identified. Upper-right label areas are
quiet at contact-sheet size. These are generated artwork candidates, not a claim
of user approval.
